A Limited Liability Partnership gives two or more people a separate legal entity with limited liability and far less ceremony than a company — no board meetings, no AGM, no statutory audit until the business is real. It is the natural home for services firms, agencies, consultancies and family trading businesses that will not sell equity to outsiders.

## Requirements

- At least **two partners**, two of whom are **designated partners** (one resident in India). Companies and LLPs can be partners; a minor cannot.
- **DPIN/DIN** for designated partners (allotted in FiLLiP for up to two).
- **Class 3 DSCs** for designated partners.
- A registered office in India with proof and NOC.
- **Contribution** — any amount; stamp duty on the agreement depends on it.

## Incorporation in four moves

1. **Name** — reserve via RUN-LLP or inside FiLLiP (two choices, ₹200). The name must end with “LLP”.
2. **FiLLiP** — the incorporation web form: partners, contribution, office proof, subscriber sheet, consent of designated partners. Fee by contribution: ₹500 (up to ₹1 lakh) to ₹5,000 (above ₹10 lakh). PAN and TAN can be applied in the same filing.
3. **Certificate of Incorporation** with the **LLPIN**, usually in 7–12 working days after a clean filing.
4. **LLP Agreement — Form 3 within 30 days.** Execute the agreement on stamp paper (duty by state and contribution; ₹500–5,000 typical) and file Form 3. Until then the LLP Act’s default rules apply (equal profit shares, equal say) — rarely what you intended. Late filing: ₹100 a day with no cap.

## What the LLP agreement must settle

- Capital contributions and whether they earn interest (deductible up to 12% p.a.).
- Profit-sharing ratio and **working-partner remuneration** (deductible only if authorised by the agreement and within section limits: ₹3 lakh or 90% of the first ₹6 lakh of book profit, then 60%).
- Decision rights, admission and exit of partners, valuation on exit, non-compete, IP ownership (assign to the LLP), dispute resolution.
- Banking authority and who signs filings.

## Annual compliance

| Filing | Due | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Form 11** — annual return of partners | 30 May | Even with no business; ₹50 fee |
| **Form 8** — statement of accounts and solvency | 30 October | Audited if turnover > ₹40 lakh **or** contribution > ₹25 lakh |
| **ITR-5** | 31 July (31 October if tax audit) | LLP is a “firm” for income tax |
| **DIR-3 KYC** for each designated partner | 30 September | DIN deactivated if missed |
| Tax audit (section 63 of the 2025 Act) | 30 September | Turnover > ₹1 crore (₹10 crore if ≤5% cash) |
| GST, TDS, EPF/ESI | Monthly/quarterly | As applicable |

LLPs maintain books on cash or accrual basis at the registered office, preserved eight years, but no statutory registers or minutes are mandated beyond what the agreement requires.

## Tax

- The LLP pays **30%** + 12% surcharge above ₹1 crore + 4% cess; there is **no 22% concessional regime** for LLPs (effective ≈ 31.2% up to ₹1 crore, 34.94% above).
- Partners’ share of profit is **exempt** in their hands; remuneration and interest are taxable as business income for the partner.
- Alternate Minimum Tax at 18.5% applies when the LLP claims specified deductions.
- **Presumptive taxation is not available** to LLPs (it is to partnership firms and proprietors).
- An LLP cannot issue ESOPs or shares; investors can only become partners or lend.

:::note
A common pattern: run the services business as an LLP, and spin the product into a private limited company later with the LLP (or the partners) as shareholders. Decide IP ownership in writing before any code or design is shared across the two.
:::

## Converting an LLP into a company

Possible under section 366 of the Companies Act (Form URC-1 with SPICe+): at least two partners, consent of all, newspaper advertisement, no pending charges unless consented. Budget 6–10 weeks and do it before a fundraising round, not during. Capital gains tax exemption on conversion is available if turnover was under ₹60 lakh in each of the three preceding years and other conditions hold; above that, plan the tax.

## Cost of running an LLP (small services firm)

Form 8 + Form 11 + ITR-5 by a CA/CS: ₹8,000–20,000; GST filing ₹6,000–24,000 a year; audit when triggered ₹10,000–30,000. Versus a private company, you save the mandatory audit and most ROC ceremony — roughly ₹20,000–40,000 a year — at the price of being unable to raise equity.
