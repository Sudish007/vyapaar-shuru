Marketplaces give a new seller demand, logistics and payments on day one — in exchange for 10–35% of the price and a tax trail on every order. Understand the trail before you list, and reconcile it every month.

## What every marketplace asks for

- **GSTIN** (GST-exempt categories like books and some unbranded food can be listed without one on most platforms, with a declaration).
- **PAN** of the entity; for proprietors, your own.
- **Bank account** in the business name (cancelled cheque).
- **Address proof** of the pickup location; **Shops & Establishment** or Udyam sometimes requested as business proof.
- **Brand**: trademark certificate or application number for Brand Registry; **brand authorisation letter** from the owner if you resell branded goods; for generic goods you list under your own brand or “Generic”.
- **GTIN/EAN barcodes** for most categories (GS1 India membership ₹4,000–10,000/year; exemptions available for private labels on Amazon).
- Category approvals (and documents) for restricted categories: food (FSSAI), cosmetics (CDSCO/ingredients list), toys (BIS), electronics (BIS CRS), jewellery (hallmark), medicines (drug licence).

Approval typically takes 2–7 days per platform.

## GST for marketplace sellers — the specifics

1. **Registration is mandatory** to sell taxable goods through an e-commerce operator, whatever your turnover (section 24). Since October 2023 unregistered persons may sell *intra-state* via an ECO with an enrolment number and conditions; platforms have been slow to support it, and Meesho/ONDC are the ones to check. Composition dealers can now sell intra-state through ECOs.
2. **TCS 0.5%** (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST, or 0.5% IGST) is collected by the platform on your **net taxable sales** and deposited against your GSTIN; accept it monthly in the TDS/TCS credit statement so it lands in your cash ledger.
3. **You** pay the GST on each sale in your GSTR-3B; the platform’s TCS is only a credit. Returns and cancellations need **credit notes**.
4. **Place of supply** is the customer’s delivery state; one GSTIN in your home state covers sales to every state. You need a GSTIN in **another state only if you store goods there** (FBA/Flipkart warehouses). Until now that meant an additional place of business there; **Rule 14B** (recommended by the 57th GST Council on 8 Oct 2026, slated for 1 Nov 2026) lets eligible sellers (goods only through TCS-collecting ECOs, output tax on B2B ≤ ₹2.5 lakh/month) declare the ECO’s warehouse as their principal place of business in that state with the platform’s consent — await the notification and the platforms’ consent workflow.
5. **Rule 14A** gives small sellers GST registration in **3 working days**.
6. Services sold through platforms (restaurants via Swiggy/Zomato, cabs, hotels under ₹7,500) are taxed in the **platform’s** hands under section 9(5) — you still register once you cross the threshold, but the platform pays the GST on those supplies.
7. E-invoicing applies to your **B2B** sales once turnover crosses ₹5 crore; most marketplace sales are B2C.

## Income tax

- Platforms deduct **0.1% TDS under section 194-O** on gross sales (5% without PAN); individuals/HUFs with gross sales under ₹5 lakh who furnish PAN are exempt. Match the credit in **AIS/26AS**.
- Your income is the sale value less platform fees, shipping and returns — book platform invoices as expenses (they carry 18% GST you can claim).
- Presumptive taxation (6% of digital receipts, up to ₹3 crore) suits small proprietors.

## Fees and economics (indicative, 2026)

| Platform | Commission (“referral”) | Other charges | Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon.in | 2–20% by category (many categories at 0% below ₹300 since 2025) | Closing fee ₹5–65, shipping/FBA fees, storage | Every 7 days |
| Flipkart | 5–25% | Collection fee, fixed fee, shipping | 7–15 days |
| Meesho | **0%** | Shipping and penalties for RTO/quality | Weekly |
| Myntra/Ajio/Nykaa | 20–35% (curated, approval needed) | Marketing contributions | Monthly |
| JioMart, Tata Cliq, Snapdeal | 5–20% | Varies | Weekly–monthly |
| Quick commerce (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart) | Brand terms: margin 20–35% + listing fees; stock bought or consigned | Slotting, marketing | Per agreement |
| **ONDC** via seller apps (Mystore, Bizom, Shopify-ONDC) | 0–5% network fees + seller-app fee | Logistics via network | Per app |

Add 18% GST on all platform fees (claimable), **returns** (apparel 25–40%), and advertising (sponsored products 5–15% of sales to rank).

## Fulfilment choices

Self-ship (your packaging, aggregator pickups), **Easy Ship/Flipkart Smart** (platform picks up), or **FBA/Flipkart Fulfilment** (your stock in their warehouse — triggers the other-state GST question above). For D2C orders use aggregators (Shiprocket, Delhivery, iThink, Pickrr) with COD and NDR management.

## Monthly reconciliation that saves tax

1. Download **settlement reports**; tie orders, returns, fees and TCS/TDS to your sales register.
2. Book sales at invoice value (platform-generated invoice carries *your* GSTIN as seller), credit notes for returns.
3. Accept TCS credits on the GST portal; claim TDS in AIS at year end.
4. Reconcile platform fee invoices (ITC) and ad invoices.
5. Track **RTO and lost shipments** — claims to platforms, and no GST on cancelled orders.

## Disclosure duties coming on 1 January 2027

Under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Amendment Rules 2026, sellers must furnish to the platform (which displays them): legal name and whether registered, address, customer-care number, website/email, **GSTIN or Udyam number**, **country of origin**, **best-before/use-by dates**, return/refund/exchange terms including **return-shipping cost**, warranty and delivery terms. Discounts must show the **lowest price of the preceding 30 days**; sellers’ names must appear on invoices in the same size as the platform’s. Prepare product-master data now.

:::tip
Open your own store in parallel (even a one-page Shopify/Dukaan) and print it on the packaging insert — the margin on a repeat customer who buys direct is the whole profit of a marketplace business.
:::
