Exporting from India has become mostly digital: DGFT, ICEGATE, the GST portal and your bank talk to each other, and the Foreign Trade Policy 2023 plus Budget 2026 removed the main friction for small e-commerce exporters. What remains is discipline — documents, realisation of payment, and claiming what you are owed.

## Registrations, in order

1. **IEC** (DGFT, ₹500, same day). Annual update April–June or it is deactivated.
2. **GST registration + LUT** (RFD-11, each financial year) so you export at 0% without paying IGST. Without a LUT you pay IGST and claim refund — slower cash.
3. **AD code registration** at every port/airport/courier terminal you will ship from, on ICEGATE with the bank’s letter.
4. **RCMC** from the relevant Export Promotion Council (APEDA for agri/processed food, EPCH handicrafts, AEPC apparel, GJEPC gems and jewellery, Pharmexcil, CAPEXIL, Plastics EPC, EEPC engineering, SEPC services; **FIEO** for multi-product) via DGFT’s e-RCMC — needed for FTP benefits and fairs.
5. Product-specific: **APEDA/Spices Board/Tea/Coffee Board** registrations; **FSSAI** for food; **phytosanitary** certificates for plant products; **drug export NOC**; **BIS/CE/FDA** depending on destination.
6. **ECGC** policy if you sell on open account; **ICEGATE** login for yourself even if a CHA files.

## Documents per shipment

Proforma invoice → purchase order → **commercial invoice** (IEC, GSTIN, LUT ARN, HS code, Incoterm, currency, buyer details) and **packing list** → **shipping bill** (filed by your CHA/courier on ICEGATE; tick **RoDTEP** and drawback) → **bill of lading / airway bill** → certificate of origin (FTA or non-preferential, from DGFT’s COO portal or chambers) → insurance → **Export General Manifest** by the carrier (proof of export for GST). After payment: **FIRA/e-FIRC** from the bank or PA and **e-BRC** self-certified on DGFT against the bank’s IRM.

Exports to Nepal/Bhutan in INR and supplies to SEZs follow special rules; “deemed exports” (to EOUs/advance-authorisation holders) earn refunds too.

## Money: realisation and FEMA

- RBI’s **Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) Regulations, 2026** (January 2026) unified the export and import rules and asked all exporters, including service exporters, to file the Export Declaration Form through their bank/portal flows.
- **Realisation period**: the January 2026 regulations allowed 15 months (18 if invoiced in INR); an **amendment dated 22 September 2026 reduced this to 9 months (12 months for INR)**. Confirm the current period with your AD bank — late realisation needs a bank extension and blocks incentives.
- Receive payments in your **current account via SWIFT**, or through RBI-regulated cross-border payment aggregators (Razorpay International, Skydo, Xflow, Payoneer, PayPal) that issue FIRAs with purpose codes (P0102 goods, P0802/P1007 etc. for services).
- Advance payments from buyers are fine; refunds of advances and write-offs need bank approval within limits (self-write-off up to 5% for status holders, else via AD bank).
- **Third-party payments** (paying someone other than the buyer, or receiving from a third party) need documentation matching the invoice.

## GST on exports

Zero-rated. Two routes: **LUT** (export without tax, claim refund of accumulated **input tax credit** in RFD-01 — from November 2026, 90% of refund claims are sanctioned provisionally by the system) or **pay IGST** and claim the refund automatically through the shipping bill (needs GSTR-1 table 6A to match ICEGATE). Keep export invoices in a separate series; the EGM must be filed for refunds to flow. Export of **services** is zero-rated when the recipient is outside India, payment is in convertible forex (or INR permitted by RBI) and you are not an intermediary.

## Incentives

| Scheme | What you get | How |
|---|---|---|
| **RoDTEP** | 0.3–4.3% of FOB as transferable e-scrips (rates per HS code; lower for AA/EOU exporters) | Declare on the shipping bill; generate scrips on ICEGATE after scroll; sell or use against BCD |
| **Duty Drawback** | Refund of customs duty on imported inputs (All-Industry Rate per HS, or brand rate) | Declare on shipping bill; credited to the AD-code bank account |
| **Advance Authorisation** | Duty-free import of inputs for export production | DGFT licence against export obligation |
| **EPCG** | Zero-duty capital goods against 6× export obligation in 6 years | DGFT licence |
| **Interest Equalisation / credit** | Subsidised pre- and post-shipment credit (scheme status changes — check Updates) | Bank |
| **Market Access Initiative, TMA** | Fair participation, freight assistance for agri | EPC/DGFT |
| **Export Promotion Mission (EPM)** (2026) | Government to bear certification costs for micro and small exporters (announced Oct 2026) | DGFT/EPCs — details via Updates |
| **ECGC** | Insurance against buyer default and political risk | Policy premium 0.1–1% |

## E-commerce and courier exports

- The **₹10 lakh per consignment cap** on courier exports is **gone** (Budget 2026 and CBIC’s 2026 amendments): ship any value by courier under the CSB-V route with your IEC, AD code and a GST invoice; incentives (RoDTEP/drawback) are available on courier shipping bills.
- **Dak Ghar Niryat Kendras** (postal export centres, 1,000+) accept parcels with electronic declaration — convenient for artisans and small brands.
- **E-Commerce Export Hubs (ECEH)** are being piloted for warehousing, returns and re-import without duty — watch the feed.
- Platforms: **Amazon Global Selling** (US, UK, UAE, Australia, Japan…), **Etsy** (handmade, vintage, craft supplies), **eBay**, **Walmart Marketplace**, **Shopify Markets** with DHL Express/FedEx/ShipGlobal/Shiprocket X; marketplaces often collect destination VAT/sales tax for you.
- **Returns**: re-import of unsold/returned goods is allowed duty-free within time limits with the original shipping bill — keep the paper trail.

## Finding buyers

Export Promotion Councils (buyer-seller meets, subsidised fairs), **Indian embassies’ commercial wings**, DGFT’s trade portal, Alibaba Gold Supplier/IndiaMART export leads, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, international fairs (Ambiente, Magic, Gulfood, Canton Fair as a visitor), sourcing agents for retail chains, and government buyer databases (APEDA’s, FIEO’s). Start with one market, one certification set, one Incoterm (FOB or CIF), and ECGC cover for anything beyond a 30% advance.

:::warning
Two silent killers: forgetting to tick RoDTEP/drawback on the shipping bill (benefit lost permanently) and letting a buyer pay late past the RBI window without a bank extension (incentive recovery with interest and a FEMA problem).
:::
