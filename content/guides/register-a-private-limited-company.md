A private limited company is the default vehicle for anything that will raise money, hire a team or hold a brand. Incorporation is fully online on the MCA V3 portal through one integrated web form, **SPICe+**, which also delivers PAN, TAN and several other registrations. Done cleanly it takes **7–15 working days** and ₹8,000–25,000 all-in.

## Requirements

- **Two directors** (one must have stayed in India 182+ days in the previous year) and **two shareholders**; the same two people can be both. An OPC needs one member plus a nominee.
- **No minimum capital.** Most founders authorise ₹1–10 lakh and pay in ₹1 lakh or less. MCA charges **no fee** for authorised capital up to ₹15 lakh; state stamp duty still applies.
- **Registered office** in India — a home address works with the owner’s NOC and a recent utility bill (under two months old).
- **Digital Signature Certificates** (Class 3) for every subscriber and director.
- A **name** that is not identical or deceptively similar to an existing company, LLP or trademark, and does not use restricted words (Bank, Insurance, National, etc.) without approvals.

## Step by step

### 1. Prepare (1–3 days)
Decide shareholding and roles; buy DSCs (₹1,000–2,500 each; eMudhra, Sify, Capricorn and other CCA-licensed CAs issue them within hours after video KYC); gather PAN, Aadhaar, passport photos, personal address proofs (bank statement or utility bill), and the office proof.

### 2. SPICe+ Part A — name (1–2 working days)
Log in to MCA V3 → SPICe+ → Part A. Propose up to two names with the activity description and NIC code. Fee ₹1,000. Approved names are reserved for 20 days. Check the trademark registry yourself first — the ROC only checks company names.

### 3. SPICe+ Part B — incorporation (file within 20 days of name approval)
One web form captures: capital and shareholding, director details (DIN is allotted here for up to three directors without one), registered office, subscriber details, and declarations. Attach:

- **e-MoA (INC-33)** and **e-AoA (INC-34)** — filled online; the main object clause should be specific but broad enough for pivots.
- **INC-9** (declaration by subscribers/directors — auto-generated) and **DIR-2** (consent to act).
- Office proof + NOC; identity/address proofs of each person.
- **AGILE-PRO-S (INC-35)**: applications for GSTIN (optional at this stage — many founders wait), EPFO and ESIC (mandatory to apply; coverage starts only when thresholds are crossed), professional tax (Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal), Shops & Establishment (some states) and the bank account.

Pay the stamp duty (varies from a few hundred rupees in some states to ₹10,000+ in others for a ₹1 lakh capital) and the form fee. The CRC (Central Registration Centre) processes it; resubmission requests are common for proof mismatches — answer within 15 days.

### 4. Certificate of Incorporation
Arrives with the **CIN**, **PAN** and **TAN** by email. Download the certificate, MoA and AoA from the portal. Your company exists from this date.

## The first 180 days — do not skip these

| Task | Deadline | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Open a current account and have subscribers pay for their shares | Before INC-20A | Share money must come from each subscriber’s own account |
| **INC-20A** declaration of commencement of business | 180 days from incorporation | Cannot borrow or start business before; penalty ₹50,000 + ₹1,000/day |
| Appoint the **first auditor** (board resolution) | 30 days from incorporation | Every company is audited; ADT-1 filed after the first AGM |
| Issue share certificates and pay stamp duty on them | 60 days from allotment | Investors check this first |
| Name board at the office; CIN on letterheads, invoices, website | Immediately | Section 12 display requirements |
| GST registration (if not taken via AGILE) | Before you need to invoice with GST | See the GST guide |
| Udyam + DPIIT recognition | Any time; early is better | Free, unlocks benefits |
| Statutory registers and minute book | Ongoing | Members, directors, charges, contracts |

## Common rejections and how to avoid them

- Utility bill older than two months or not in the owner’s name → get a fresh bill and a notarised NOC.
- Name too close to a registered trademark in a related class → search IP India first.
- Object clause copied from another company and inconsistent with the NIC code.
- Subscriber signatures not matching PAN (middle names) → use the PAN name everywhere.
- Director already holding a DIN that is deactivated for missed DIR-3 KYC → fix KYC first.

:::warning
Nobody from “MCA” will call you to sell incorporation. Use the mca.gov.in portal yourself or a CA/CS you have met. Fees quoted above are indicative; the fee calculator on the portal is authoritative.
:::

## OPC specifics

An OPC is a private company with one member. Add nominee consent (INC-3) to Part B; no AGM is required; annual filings are AOC-4 and MGT-7A. Only an Indian resident natural person can incorporate one, and a person can hold only one OPC at a time. Since 2021 there are no turnover or capital ceilings forcing conversion.
