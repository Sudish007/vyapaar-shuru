Licences in India come from three levels — **central** (FSSAI, BIS, CDSCO, DGFT), **state** (Shops & Establishment, professional tax, pollution boards, factories, excise) and **municipal** (trade licence, signage, health). Most businesses need two to four of them. Use this directory to find yours, then the National Single Window System or your state single window to apply.

## Almost everyone

### Shops and Establishments registration (state)
Every shop, office, warehouse, godown, restaurant or commercial establishment registers with the state labour department, usually **within 30 days** of starting; the certificate also serves as a business proof. Online in most states (e.g., Maharashtra’s portal exempts establishments under 10 workers from the fee; Karnataka, Delhi, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat and others have their own forms). Cost ₹0–5,000 by headcount; renewal every 1–5 years depending on state. It governs working hours, weekly off, leave and holidays for your staff.

### Professional tax (state)
Levied by Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Kerala, Assam, Bihar and others (not Delhi, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan). The entity enrols (PTEC, ₹2,500 a year typically) and employers register (PTRC) to deduct from salaries; monthly or annual returns.

### Udyam (MSME)
Free, instant, and the key to subsidies and the 45-day payment rule — see its own guide.

### Trademark
Not a licence but treat it like one: file before launch. See the IP guide.

## Physical premises

### Trade licence (municipal)
Shops, eateries, workshops, warehouses and many service premises need a licence from the municipal corporation or panchayat under the state municipal act. Documents: premises proof, property-tax receipt, owner NOC, ID, sometimes fire NOC and layout. ₹500–10,000 a year by trade and city; renew annually (many cities auto-renew on payment). Eating houses in Delhi, Mumbai and other metros also need a police **eating-house licence**.

### Signage / advertisement permission (municipal)
Illuminated or large signboards need municipal permission and attract an annual advertisement tax in metros.

### Fire NOC (state fire service)
Required for buildings above height/area thresholds, assembly spaces, restaurants above seating limits, warehouses with hazardous goods, factories, hotels, hospitals and schools. Provisional NOC before fit-out, final after inspection; renewal 1–3 years.

### Lease registration
Leases over 11 months must be **registered** (stamp duty + registration fee, state-wise) to be enforceable; unregistered long leases are the classic landlord-dispute loss.

## Food

### FSSAI registration or licence (FoSCoS)
Mandatory for every food business operator — manufacturers, packers, caterers, restaurants, cloud and home kitchens, retailers, wholesalers, transporters, storage, importers, e-commerce food sellers and delivery platforms. **From 1 April 2026**: turnover up to **₹1.5 crore** → Basic Registration (₹100/year); ₹1.5–50 crore → **State licence** (₹2,000–5,000/year by kind of business); above ₹50 crore, and **all importers and e-commerce FBOs** → **Central licence** (₹7,500/year). Validity 1–5 years; renew 30 days before expiry. Display the 14-digit number on the premises and on every label and online listing, follow the labelling regulations (veg/non-veg mark, nutrition panel, allergens, dates) and file the annual return (D1) if licensed in manufacturing. Co-packers must hold their own licence; the brand owner’s number goes on the label.

Related: **Legal Metrology** (below) for packaged food; **APEDA/Spices Board/Tea Board** registration for exports; **excise licence** for anything alcoholic (state, expensive, slow).

## Products and manufacturing

### Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules
Every pre-packed product carries declarations — name and address of manufacturer/packer/importer, common name, net quantity, **MRP inclusive of all taxes**, month and year of manufacture/import, consumer-care contact, and country of origin for imports. Manufacturers and packers register with the **state** Controller of Legal Metrology; importers register with the **central** Director (Rule 27) before importing; ₹500–2,000. Weighing scales and measures used in trade need stamped verification. The 2026 amendments extend country-of-origin display duties to online listings. Penalties are per offence and marketplaces delist non-compliant SKUs.

### BIS — ISI mark, CRS, hallmarking
Products under a **Quality Control Order** (cement, steel, electrical appliances, toys, footwear, pressure cookers, helmets, water bottles, many textiles) need the ISI mark; **electronics and IT goods** (phones, laptops, LED lights, power banks, chargers, smart watches…) need **Compulsory Registration Scheme** registration — for imports, the *foreign manufacturer* holds the registration. Gold and silver jewellery must be **hallmarked** (BIS registration ₹2,000–7,500 by turnover). Timelines 2–4 months; testing at BIS-recognised labs; fees from ₹25,000 to several lakh.

### Pollution consents (State Pollution Control Board)
Industries are classified **White** (no consent; online intimation), **Green**, **Orange** and **Red**. Green/Orange/Red need **Consent to Establish** before setting up and **Consent to Operate** before production, via the state OCMMS portal, with fees by capital investment and validity of 5–15 years for Green/Orange. Also: hazardous-waste authorisation, e-waste/plastic-waste **EPR registration** on the CPCB portal for producers, importers and brand owners of electronics, batteries, plastic packaging — increasingly enforced for D2C brands.

### Factory licence (state Directorate of Factories)
Premises with a manufacturing process and **10+ workers with power** (20 without) are a “factory”: building-plan approval, licence from the Chief Inspector, annual renewal, safety and welfare rules, Factory Act registers. Under the OSH Code the thresholds are 20/40 once states notify the rules — check your state.

### Drugs, cosmetics, medical devices (CDSCO / state drug controller)
Manufacturing or importing cosmetics needs a CDSCO licence/import registration (cosmetics: Form COS); pharmacies need a retail drug licence (state) with a registered pharmacist; medical devices are risk-classified under the MDR 2017 with licences/registrations; nutraceuticals sit under FSSAI. Ayurvedic/herbal products need a state AYUSH licence.

### Others
Explosives/fireworks (PESO), LPG/petroleum storage (PESO), boilers (state), electrical contractors (state licence), drones (DGCA), telecom/ISP (DoT), security agencies (PSARA), travel agents (IATA/ministry recognition), schools (state education department/affiliation), hospitals and clinics (**Clinical Establishments Act** registration in adopting states, biomedical waste authorisation, PCPNDT for ultrasound), gyms and salons (municipal health trade licences), playing recorded music in a café or store (**PPL/IPRS** licences), lending or payments (**RBI** NBFC/PA licence), investment advice (**SEBI**).

## How to apply efficiently

1. List the applicable licences from this page and confirm against your state single window (Maharashtra MAITRI, Karnataka Udyog Mitra/eBiz, Tamil Nadu TNSWP, Telangana TG-iPASS, Gujarat IFP, UP Nivesh Mitra, Delhi DSIIDC/labour portal, West Bengal Silpasathi).
2. Apply for premises-dependent licences (S&E, trade, fire) the week you sign the lease; sector licences (FSSAI, BIS, CDSCO) run in parallel with incorporation.
3. Keep a licence register: number, issue date, expiry, renewal window, portal login — and feed it into the compliance calendar.

:::warning
Agents selling “all licences in one package” often file the wrong category (e.g., FSSAI registration where a licence is needed) because the cheaper category is faster. Read the eligibility yourself — the kind-of-business tables on FoSCoS and BIS are public.
:::
