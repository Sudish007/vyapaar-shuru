On **21 November 2025** the four Labour Codes — Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions — replaced 29 central laws, with a one-year transition for existing registrations ending **20 November 2026** and states notifying their own rules through 2026. For a new employer the practical effect is a cleaner rulebook with the same triggers you had to watch before, plus a new definition of “wages” that changes how salaries are structured.

## Headcount triggers

| Employees | What kicks in |
|---|---|
| **1** | Written contract (good practice; required for fixed-term), minimum wages, payment of wages by the 7th, salary TDS, professional tax (levying states), Shops & Establishment registration, equal remuneration |
| **10** | **ESI** (wages ≤ ₹21,000; ₹25,000 for persons with disability; 20 in some states), **gratuity** (after 5 years’ service; fixed-term staff pro-rata after 1 year), **maternity benefit** (26 weeks paid; crèche at 50), **POSH** Internal Committee, Factories/OSH registration for manufacturing with power |
| **20** | **EPF** (12% + 12%; EDLI 0.5%; admin 0.5%), **bonus** (8.33–20% for wages ≤ ₹21,000), contract-labour licence for principal employers, standing orders at 300 (IR Code), notice of change rules |
| **50** | Crèche, welfare officer thresholds (state rules), Employees’ Compensation nuances |
| **100 / 300** | Lay-off/retrenchment permissions (300 under the IR Code) |

Count **everyone** — interns on stipends, part-timers, contract staff through agencies (for ESI/EPF the principal employer is liable if the contractor defaults).

## The new “wages” definition

All four Codes use one definition: basic + dearness allowance + retaining allowance, and **if excluded components (HRA, conveyance, bonus, overtime, commissions, etc.) exceed 50% of total remuneration, the excess is added back**. In effect, at least **50% of CTC counts as wages** for PF, gratuity, leave encashment and bonus — restructuring salaries to a 30% basic no longer works. Expect PF and gratuity costs to rise 5–10% for low-basic structures.

## Statutory contributions

| Scheme | Employee | Employer | Due | Portal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EPF** (wage ceiling ₹15,000 for mandatory coverage; voluntary above) | 12% | 12% (3.67% EPF + 8.33% EPS) + 0.5% EDLI + 0.5% admin | 15th of next month (ECR) | EPFO unified portal |
| **ESI** (wages ≤ ₹21,000) | 0.75% | 3.25% | 15th | ESIC |
| **Professional tax** | ₹200/month typical (state slabs) | Nil (plus entity PTEC ₹2,500/yr) | Monthly/annual by state | State portal |
| **Gratuity** | — | 4.81% of basic accrued (pay on exit after 5 years; 15 days’ wages per year) | On exit | — |
| **Bonus** | — | 8.33–20% of wages (≤ ₹21,000) | Within 8 months of FY end | — |
| **Labour Welfare Fund** | ₹6–50/yr | Matching | Half-yearly/annual by state | State |

Register for **EPF and ESI even before thresholds** if staff ask — voluntary coverage is allowed and many startups do it for recruiting. Once registered, contributions are mandatory for all eligible staff.

## Payroll TDS (section 192)

Collect each employee’s regime choice (default new regime unless they opt out) and investment declarations; deduct monthly on the projected annual salary; deposit by the **7th**; file **24Q** quarterly; issue **Form 16** by **15 June**. Perquisites (ESOPs, rent-free accommodation, car) are taxable; reimbursements need bills.

## Contracts and policies

- **Offer letter + employment agreement**: role, CTC with components, probation (3–6 months), notice (30–90 days), confidentiality, **IP assignment** (essential — without it the employee owns inventions outside “course of employment” grey zones), non-solicit (enforceable), non-compete after exit (largely **unenforceable** in India), POSH acknowledgement, leave policy per state S&E Act (typically 12–21 earned leave, 12 casual/sick).
- **Fixed-term employment** is now explicitly allowed with equal benefits and pro-rata gratuity after one year.
- **Consultants/contractors**: a contract-for-services with deliverables, 194J/194C TDS, their own GST. Courts look at control and exclusivity — a full-time “consultant” with your email ID and fixed hours is an employee for EPF/ESI/gratuity.
- **Interns**: stipend is taxable salary if an employer–employee relationship exists; AICTE/university interns on structured programmes are usually outside EPF/ESI, but register them for ESI if they do regular work.
- **Policies** (write once): leave, work hours (OSH Code: 8–12-hour day within 48-hour week; overtime at double rate), remote work, expense reimbursement, anti-harassment (POSH), code of conduct, data protection (DPDP applies to employee data too).

## POSH (10+ employees)

Written policy, **Internal Committee** (presiding woman officer, two members, one external member from an NGO/legal background), display at the workplace, annual awareness sessions, **annual report** to the District Officer by 31 January, and disclosure in the directors’ report for companies. Fine ₹50,000 and licence cancellation on repeat default.

## Registrations checklist for a new employer

- [ ] Shops & Establishment (state), within 30 days of opening
- [ ] Professional tax PTEC/PTRC (levying states)
- [ ] TAN and salary TDS setup
- [ ] EPF (20+) and ESI (10+) on Shram Suvidha / portals; UAN and IP numbers for staff
- [ ] POSH IC and policy (10+)
- [ ] Labour Welfare Fund (state)
- [ ] Registers: wages, attendance, leave, overtime, fines/deductions (electronic allowed) — the Codes consolidate them into common forms
- [ ] Display: abstract of the Codes/S&E Act, working hours, holidays, POSH order, grievance officer

## Hiring remote or foreign

Employees in another state → that state’s S&E registration and professional tax may apply; EPF/ESI are central. Hiring abroad without a foreign entity means an Employer of Record (Deel, Remote, Multiplier) — treat as a vendor with 195 TDS analysis. Foreign nationals working in India need an Employment Visa and FRRO registration; their EPF is mandatory as “international workers” with no wage ceiling unless a social-security agreement applies.
