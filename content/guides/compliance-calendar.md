Compliance is not a pile of forms; it is a **rhythm**. Once the recurring dates are in one calendar with owners and reminders, a founder spends two to four hours a month on it. The interactive **Calendar** page builds the dated list for your structure and registrations; this page explains the rhythm.

## Monthly

| Date | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 7th | Anyone with a TAN | Deposit last month’s TDS/TCS |
| 10th | ECOs | GSTR-8 (platforms only) |
| 11th | GST monthly filers | GSTR-1 |
| 13th | QRMP filers | IFF (optional) in months 1–2 |
| 15th | Employers | EPF ECR + payment; ESI payment |
| 20th | GST monthly filers | GSTR-3B + tax |
| 25th | QRMP filers | PMT-06 tax in months 1–2 |
| State date | Employers in PT states | Professional tax |
| Any day | Everyone | Bank reconciliation; IMS invoice actions; e-way bills; books closed for the month |

## Quarterly

- **Advance tax**: 15 June (15%), 15 September (45%), 15 December (75%), 15 March (100%; presumptive taxpayers all at once).
- **TDS returns** 24Q/26Q/27Q: 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May; TCS 27EQ: 15 July/October/January/May; Form 16A within 15 days after.
- **QRMP**: GSTR-1 (13th) and GSTR-3B (22nd/24th) after each quarter; **CMP-08** (18th) for composition.
- Board meeting (small companies: at least one in each half-year, 90 days apart).

## Yearly — the April–March map

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **April** | New invoice series; LUT for the year; QRMP/composition options; **IEC update window opens**; MSME-1 (30 Apr); GSTR-4 (30 Apr) | All / exporters / companies / composition |
| **May** | LLP Form 11 (30 May); TDS Q4 returns (31 May) | LLPs / deductors |
| **June** | Form 16 (15 Jun); DPT-3 (30 Jun); **IEC update closes (30 Jun)**; advance tax 1 | Employers / companies / exporters |
| **July** | ITR for non-audit individuals, firms, LLPs (31 Jul); FLA return (15 Jul) if foreign investment/ODI | Proprietors, firms, LLPs |
| **September** | Advance tax 2; **AGM (30 Sep)**; **DIR-3 KYC (30 Sep)**; **tax audit report (30 Sep)** | Companies, LLPs, audit cases |
| **October** | ADT-1 (15 Oct); **AOC-4 (30 Oct)**; **LLP Form 8 (30 Oct)**; **ITR for companies and audit cases (31 Oct)**; MSME-1 (31 Oct) | Companies, LLPs |
| **November** | **MGT-7/7A (29 Nov)**; ITR with transfer pricing (30 Nov) | Companies |
| **December** | Advance tax 3; **GSTR-9/9C (31 Dec)**; ODI APR (31 Dec); belated/revised ITR (31 Dec; Budget 2026 proposes 31 March with fee) | GST > ₹2 crore / companies with ODI |
| **January** | POSH annual report (31 Jan); TDS Q3 | Employers |
| **March** | Advance tax 4 (15 Mar); composition opt-in (31 Mar); renewals of licences expiring at year end | All |

Plus **event-driven**: INC-20A (180 days from incorporation), ADT-1 for first auditor, PAS-3 within 15 days of allotment, CHG-1 within 30 days of a charge, FC-GPR within 30 days of allotting to foreigners, REG-14 within 15 days of a GST detail change, SOFTEX within 30 days of a software export invoice, e-BRC within the realisation window, trademark renewal every 10 years, FSSAI/trade licence/S&E renewals on their own cycles, DSC renewal every two years.

## Who owns what (a two-person team)

- **Founder/finance owner**: bank reconciliation, invoice approvals, advance tax decisions, signing filings.
- **Accountant or CA (retainer ₹3,000–15,000 a month for a small company)**: GST and TDS returns, payroll statutory filings, books, year-end.
- **CS/CA for ROC**: the annual company forms and event filings.
- **Payroll/accounting software**: generates the data; **calendar with two reminders** (7 days and 1 day before) catches the dates.

## Automate and simplify

- Pick one accounting system that files GSTR-1/3B and reconciles 2B; one payroll system that files PF/ESI/PT/TDS.
- Use QRMP if under ₹5 crore — same tax, fewer returns.
- Opt for presumptive taxation as a proprietor if it fits — it removes books and audit.
- Keep a **licence and registration register** with expiry dates; renew 30 days early.
- Review the **Updates** feed weekly for due-date extensions and new forms (they are frequent, especially at year-end).

:::warning
The expensive misses are rarely tax: ROC forms (₹100/day, uncapped), unfiled LLP forms, lapsed DIR-3 KYC, IEC deactivation in July, and GST returns older than three years that can no longer be filed. Calendar those first.
:::
