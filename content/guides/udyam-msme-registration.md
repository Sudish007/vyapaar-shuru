Udyam is the Government of India’s free MSME registry. Over 7.4 crore enterprises had registered by the end of 2025, because the certificate is the key that opens most small-business benefits — and because it costs nothing and takes a quarter of an hour.

## Are you an MSME? (definition since 1 April 2025)

| Category | Investment in plant, machinery or equipment | **and** turnover |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | up to ₹2.5 crore | up to ₹10 crore |
| **Small** | up to ₹25 crore | up to ₹100 crore |
| **Medium** | up to ₹125 crore | up to ₹500 crore |

Both limits must be met; crossing either moves you up (with a grace period before benefits change). Investment is taken from your ITR (written-down value, excluding land, building and pollution-control equipment); turnover excludes exports and GST. Traders, manufacturers and service providers all qualify (wholesale/retail trade was brought back in 2021 for priority-sector lending). Companies, LLPs, partnerships, proprietors, trusts and societies can all register.

## How to register

1. udyamregistration.gov.in → “For new entrepreneurs who are not registered yet”.
2. Aadhaar of the proprietor / managing partner / authorised director + OTP; PAN of the entity (mandatory); GSTIN where you are registered for GST.
3. Fill: name, type of organisation, NIC codes (up to several activities), number of employees, bank account, investment and turnover (self-declared; auto-verified from ITR/GST later).
4. Download the **Udyam Registration Certificate** (URN like UDYAM-MH-00-0000000) — instant, with a QR code.

Update annually if details change; the portal re-classifies you from ITR/GST data each year. The only official site is udyamregistration.gov.in; paid lookalikes charge ₹1,000–3,000 for the same thing. Informal micro units without PAN/GST can use **Udyam Assist** through banks and NBFCs.

## What the certificate unlocks

### Money
- **Priority-sector lending**: banks must lend a share of their book to MSMEs; MSE loans up to ₹10 lakh are collateral-free by RBI guideline.
- **CGTMSE** guarantee on loans up to ₹10 crore (higher coverage for women, NE, SC/ST); banks lend without collateral because the Trust guarantees 75–85%.
- **MUDRA** (up to ₹20 lakh with Tarun Plus), **PMEGP** subsidy (15–35%), **Stand-Up India**, state capital/interest subsidies, the **₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund** (Budget 2026) and the **MSME credit card** (₹5 lakh, Budget 2025) all key off Udyam.
- **TReDS**: discount invoices on large buyers; companies with turnover above ₹250 crore must onboard.
- Lower interest spreads and faster processing at most PSU banks for Udyam holders.

### Getting paid
- **45-day rule**: buyers must pay micro and small suppliers within the agreed period, maximum 45 days (15 without agreement). Interest at **three times the bank rate**, compounded monthly, accrues automatically; buyers lose the tax deduction until they pay (section 43B(h) regime continued in the 2025 Act). Companies must report overdue dues in **MSME-1**.
- **MSME Samadhaan**: file a claim online; the Facilitation Council must decide within 90 days.

### Markets
- **Public procurement**: 25% of central purchases reserved for MSEs (4% SC/ST, 3% women), exemption from EMD/tender fees, price preference and free GeM/NSIC registration.
- **GeM** seller preference; **NSIC** single-point registration for government tenders.

### Fees and compliance
- **50% off trademark** filing (₹4,500 instead of ₹9,000 per class) and patent fee rebates.
- ISO/quality certification reimbursement schemes, ZED certification subsidies, barcode subsidies via NSIC.
- Protection from “deemed deposit” rules and lighter inspection regimes under several state laws.
- Electricity and stamp-duty concessions in many states for Udyam-registered units.

## The MSMED (Amendment) Bill, 2026

Introduced in 2026, the amendment proposes a phased rollout of changes to the Act (classification mechanics, dispute resolution and facilitation). Read its status in the Updates feed before relying on provisions that may change; the registration and benefits above are current as of October 2026.

:::tip
Register **before** you need a loan or a tender. Banks look at the Udyam date; some schemes require 6–12 months of registration. It costs nothing to be early.
:::

## Common mistakes

- Registering with the wrong NIC code (e.g., trading when you manufacture) — affects scheme eligibility.
- Not adding GSTIN after registration — the portal expects it once you are registered for GST.
- Forgetting the Udyam number on invoices to large buyers — the 45-day rule bites only if the buyer knows (put “Udyam No.” on every invoice).
- Paying an agent for a “renewal” — Udyam never expires.
