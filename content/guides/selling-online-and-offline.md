Each sales channel carries its own compliance kit: an online store needs legal pages and GST-correct checkout, a shop needs premises licences and a GST-ready POS, B2B needs invoice discipline and the 45-day MSME rule, and government buyers need GeM registration. This guide is the practical layer on top of the GST and licences guides.

## Your own online store

### Platform
Shopify (₹1,994–7,447/month plans; Indian gateways via apps), WooCommerce (free + hosting), Dukaan/Instamojo/Wix (quick starts), or headless stacks later. Pick one that supports **GST per product (HSN), tax-inclusive pricing, invoice generation with your GSTIN, state capture at checkout and COD**.

### Checkout and invoicing
- Show **MRP/tax-inclusive prices** for B2C; the invoice must carry your GSTIN, HSN, tax split (CGST+SGST for same-state buyers, IGST otherwise) and **place of supply** (delivery state).
- Capture the **billing state** (place of supply) and, for B2B, the buyer’s GSTIN for a tax invoice.
- Collect payments via an RBI-regulated gateway (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe PG, Paytm) — see banking; COD via your courier aggregator.
- Issue **credit notes** for returns within the GST timelines; refunds within the promised window.

### Legal pages (required by gateways, the IT Rules, Consumer Protection rules and DPDP)
Terms of use · Privacy notice (DPDP-ready: what, why, how long, rights, grievance contact) · Refund/return/cancellation policy · Shipping policy · Contact page with **legal name, address, email, phone** · **Grievance officer** name and contact (acknowledge in 48 hours, resolve in a month) · Pricing/transaction currency · Country of origin on product pages for imports.

### Shipping
Aggregators (Shiprocket, Delhivery Direct, iThink Logistics, Pickrr, Nimbuspost) give multi-courier rates, COD remittance and NDR tools; e-way bills are needed for shipments above ₹50,000; keep **proof of delivery** for chargebacks.

### Packaging and labels
Legal Metrology declarations on every pre-packed item (see licences); FSSAI logo and number on food; BIS/ISI marks where mandatory; care labels on textiles (fibre content), size, and the **country of origin**.

## A physical outlet

- Lease (registered if > 11 months), **Shops & Establishment**, **trade licence**, **signage permission**, **fire NOC** where applicable, FSSAI for anything edible, drug licence for a pharmacy, music licences if you play music, weights and measures stamping for scales.
- **POS billing**: software that prints GST invoices (B2C invoices under ₹200 can be consolidated), tracks inventory and HSN; **UPI QR** from your bank/PA; card terminals (MDR ~1–2%).
- Display: GST certificate, S&E certificate, FSSAI licence, trade licence, “no refund/exchange” terms if any (must be reasonable under consumer law), MRP on every item.
- Cash: receiving ₹2 lakh or more from one person in cash is penalised (section 269ST); cash purchases above ₹10,000 are not deductible.
- Staff: offer letters, PT, ESI/EPF at thresholds, attendance and wage registers under the Codes.

## B2B selling

- **Tax invoices** with buyer GSTIN; **e-invoicing** once your turnover crosses ₹5 crore; **e-way bills** above ₹50,000.
- **Credit terms**: if you are a micro/small enterprise, print your Udyam number on invoices — buyers must pay within 45 days or lose the deduction and owe interest; if you *buy* from MSMEs, the same applies to you.
- **TDS by buyers**: large buyers (turnover > ₹10 crore) deduct 0.1% under 194Q on purchases above ₹50 lakh a year from you; reconcile in AIS.
- **Contracts**: purchase orders or MSAs with delivery, acceptance, payment terms, late-payment interest, IP, confidentiality, jurisdiction (see contracts guide).
- **Invoice financing** on TReDS or fintech lenders once you sell to large companies.
- Platforms: IndiaMART/TradeIndia (leads), Udaan/Jumbotail (distribution), Amazon Business (B2B with GST invoices), Moglix/Zetwerk (industrial).

## Selling to government — GeM

The **Government e-Marketplace** is mandatory for central purchases and used by most states and PSUs. Register as a seller with PAN, GSTIN (exempt sellers allowed with declaration), bank account, Udyam (gets you exemptions from EMD, prior turnover and experience criteria), and ITR history. List products against GeM categories, respond to bids, deliver, and get paid through the buyer’s PFMS flow (payment timelines of 10 days after acceptance are mandated; delays are common — use **GeM Sahay** financing or TReDS). Also: **NSIC** single-point registration for tenders outside GeM, state procurement preferences (25% MSE reservation).

## ONDC and social commerce

- **ONDC**: list once through a seller-side app (Mystore, Bizom, Shopify’s connector, Snapdeal’s ONDC app, state-run apps); your catalogue appears on buyer apps (Paytm, Magicpin, Mystore, PhonePe Pincode, Ola…). Commission 0–5% plus logistics; same GST rules as any ECO (TCS applies through the seller app).
- **Instagram/WhatsApp**: WhatsApp Business API with catalogues and payments (UPI), Instagram Shops via Meta Commerce Manager. Legally identical to your own store — invoices, policies, DPDP consent for messaging.
- **Live commerce and influencers**: influencer payments attract TDS (194J/194H/194C) and GST (influencer must charge 18% if registered); the **ASCI guidelines** require disclosure of paid promotions; CCPA fines for misleading ads fall on the brand too.

## Franchising

India has no franchise statute; the agreement governs everything: territory, fees (franchise fee + royalty with 18% GST), term and renewal, brand standards, supply obligations, termination and non-compete. Trademark registration is the franchisor’s base; the franchisee needs its own GST, S&E, FSSAI etc. Register the trademark licence (optional but useful).

:::tip
Whatever the channel, create one **product master** with HSN, GST rate, MRP, net quantity, country of origin, best-before logic, manufacturer/packer text and barcode — the same data feeds your store, marketplaces, GeM and labels, and the 2027 e-commerce disclosure rules require most of it.
:::
