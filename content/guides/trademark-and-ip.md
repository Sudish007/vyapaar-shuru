Your brand name is the one asset that gets more valuable the longer you trade — and the one you can lose overnight if someone else registered it first. Trademark filing is cheap, online and should happen **before** you print packaging or open marketplace accounts.

## Trademarks

### What can be registered
Words, logos, taglines, shapes, colours, sounds — anything distinctive that identifies your goods or services. Descriptive words (“Best Tea”), surnames alone, geographic names and generic terms are hard or impossible to register; coined words (“Zomato”, “Nykaa”) are easiest.

### Classes
Goods and services are grouped in **45 Nice classes**; you pay per class. Common picks: 3 cosmetics · 5 pharma and supplements · 9 software and electronics · 25 apparel · 29/30 food · 32 beverages · 35 retail, online marketplace, advertising · 41 education · 42 software as a service, IT · 43 restaurants · 44 health and beauty services. A D2C apparel brand files 25 + 35; a SaaS files 9 + 42.

### Search first
Use the **public search** on ipindia.gov.in (wordmark, phonetic and Vienna-code searches) in your classes and neighbours; check company names on MCA and domains. A ₹2,000–5,000 attorney search report is worth it before you commit to packaging.

### Filing (TM-A)
- Online with a DSC or through an agent.
- **Government fee per class**: **₹4,500** for individuals, DPIIT-recognised startups and Udyam MSMEs (attach the certificate — no concession without it at filing); **₹9,000** for other companies/LLPs. Physical filing costs 10% more.
- Attorney fees ₹3,000–8,000 per application.
- Claim “proposed to be used” or a date of first use with evidence (invoices, ads).

### After filing
1. **Application number** the same day — you may use **™** immediately.
2. **Examination** in roughly 3–12 months; objections (similar marks, descriptiveness) are answered with a reply and, if needed, a hearing.
3. **Publication** in the Trade Marks Journal; four months for oppositions.
4. **Registration** — typically **12–24 months** after filing; use **®** only now. Valid **10 years** from the filing date, renewable indefinitely (TM-R, ₹9,000/₹10,000 per class).

Startups and MSMEs can request **expedited examination** (TM-M, higher fee) to get an examination report within weeks.

### Using it
Register the mark with **Amazon Brand Registry** (a pending application now qualifies in India), Flipkart and Meesho brand programmes, and Instagram/Meta brand rights — this is how you remove counterfeits and hijacked listings. Record the mark with **Indian Customs (IPR recordation on ICEGATE)** if imports of fakes are a risk. Licence it to franchisees in writing.

## Copyright

Arises automatically on creation (code, website text, product photos, designs, course videos) — registration (₹500 per literary/software work; ₹2,000 for artistic works used on goods) is optional but makes enforcement far easier and is routine for software and content businesses. Make sure copyright in work done by **employees** vests in the company (employment contract) and that **contractors and agencies assign** it in writing — by default the freelancer owns what they made for you.

## Patents

For technical inventions that are new, inventive and industrially useful; software “as such” is not patentable in India, but technical effects implemented in software can be. Filing fee ₹1,600 (individual/startup/MSME, e-filing) to ₹8,000; total cost with drafting ₹60,000–2 lakh; grant 2–4 years (12–18 months with the startup expedited route). DPIIT startups get an 80% fee rebate. File **before** you publish or sell — public disclosure kills novelty.

## Designs

The look of a product (bottle shape, UI icon set, furniture) can be registered under the Designs Act for 10 (+5) years; ₹1,000 for individuals/startups/MSMEs. Cheap protection for D2C hardware and packaging.

## Geographical indications

Producers of GI-tagged goods (Darjeeling tea, Kanchipuram silk, Banarasi sarees, Alphonso mangoes…) can register as **authorised users** — a strong marker for exports.

## Year-one IP checklist

- [ ] Trademark search done in the right classes
- [ ] TM-A filed with the startup/MSME concession
- [ ] Domain names and social handles secured
- [ ] Founders’ and employees’ IP assigned to the company in writing
- [ ] Contractor agreements with assignment and moral-rights waivers
- [ ] Copyright registration for software/core content
- [ ] Patentable inventions identified before launch; NDA before demos
- [ ] Brand Registry on marketplaces once the TM application exists
