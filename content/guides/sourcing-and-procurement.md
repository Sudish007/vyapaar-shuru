Margins are made when you buy. India has deep product clusters for almost everything a consumer brand or trader sells, and imports fill the gaps — mostly electronics, machinery and components. This guide is a map plus a procurement checklist.

## Domestic clusters by product

|---|---|---|
| Knitwear, t-shirts, innerwear | **Tirupur** (TN), Ludhiana (hosiery, woollens) | MOQs from 300–500 pcs per style; export-grade factories |
| Woven apparel, denim | Delhi NCR (Noida, Gurugram, Okhla), Bengaluru, Ahmedabad (denim) | Export surplus and job-work units |

Wholesale markets for traders: **Sadar Bazar, Gandhi Nagar, Karol Bagh, Nehru Place** (Delhi); **Crawford Market, Masjid Bunder, Dadar** (Mumbai); **Chickpet, SP Road** (Bengaluru); **Burrabazar** (Kolkata); **T Nagar, Ritchie Street** (Chennai); **Begum Bazaar** (Hyderabad); **Surat textile markets**; **Yiwu-style**: Delhi’s Sadar is the closest.

## B2B platforms

**IndiaMART** and **TradeIndia** (leads, verified suppliers), **Udaan** and **Jumbotail** (distribution with credit for retailers), **Moglix** (industrial), **Amazon Business** (GST invoices), **Zetwerk** (contract manufacturing), **Fashinza/Qalara** (apparel and crafts sourcing for export). For imports: **Alibaba** (Trade Assurance escrow), **1688** (domestic Chinese wholesale — needs an agent), **Made-in-China**, **Global Sources**, **DHgate** (small lots). Fairs: **Canton Fair** (Apr/Oct), **Yiwu**, **Hong Kong Electronics Fair**, **IHGF Delhi Fair** (handicrafts), **Bharat Tex**, **AAHAR** (food), **India International Trade Fair**.

## Contract manufacturing in India

Find units via clusters, IndiaMART, Zetwerk or exporters with spare capacity. Sign a **manufacturing agreement**: specs and tolerances, materials and tests, MOQ and lead time, pricing and revision triggers, tooling ownership, exclusivity (optional), IP and confidentiality, defect and recall liability, payment terms (30% advance common), inspection rights. For food/cosmetics the manufacturer holds the FSSAI/CDSCO licence; **your brand** goes on the label with their licence and your Udyam/FSSAI as brand owner where required.

## Procurement checklist (domestic)

1. **Supplier KYC**: GSTIN (verify on the GST portal — active, filing regularly), Udyam, bank in the same name, factory photos/video, references.
2. **Samples** (pay for them), then a **signed spec sheet** and golden sample.
3. **GST invoice** for everything — no credit without it; ensure the supplier files GSTR-1 so your 2B shows it; pay within **180 days** to keep credit, within **45 days** to Udyam suppliers.
4. **E-way bill** on movements above ₹50,000; delivery challans for job work.
5. **Quality**: AQL sampling at pre-shipment; third-party inspection for large orders (₹8,000–20,000 per visit).
6. **Payment**: advance 30–50% for new suppliers via bank transfer (cash above ₹10,000 is not deductible); never full advance without a track record.

## Importing — what to decide before paying

- **Landed cost**: unit price + freight + insurance + **BCD + 10% SWS + IGST (creditable)** + clearing + local transport ± FTA savings. Use the duty tool.
- **Compliance**: BIS (electronics via the factory’s registration), FSSAI, CDSCO, Legal Metrology labels (apply at origin), EPR for plastics/electronics, anti-dumping checks, import licences.
- **Incoterms**: **FOB** with your own forwarder gives control; **EXW** for small lots via a consolidator; avoid **DDP** quotes (duty surprises, customs liability in someone else’s name).
- **Payment**: 30% TT advance + 70% against B/L copy is standard; **LC** for large orders; Alibaba Trade Assurance for new suppliers; pay only through your AD bank with the invoice (import payments are matched to Bills of Entry).
- **Supplier verification**: business licence, factory audit (SGS/QIMA/TÜV/Bureau Veritas, US$300–600), video call on the floor, test order first.
- **Lead times**: 30–45 days production + 25–40 days sea (China–Nhava Sheva/Chennai/Mundra) + 5–10 days clearance; air 5–10 days total at 3–5× cost.

## Government help for buyers

**NSIC Raw Material Assistance** (credit for bulk purchase), **MSME cluster development** programmes, **GeM** for buying office supplies compliantly, **Khadi/handloom** sourcing via KVIC and state emporia for crafts brands, **ODOP** (One District One Product) directories by state.

:::warning
A supplier who will not issue a GST invoice is costing you 18% of input credit and, if caught, your deduction — the “cash discount” is rarely worth it. And an exporter who asks you to pay a different company than the one on the invoice is a red flag for both fraud and FEMA.
:::
